🍛 Party Jollof Rice

A delicious Nigerian favourite, perfect for parties, celebrations and family gatherings.

🛒 Ingredients

  • 2 cups of rice
  • 4 tomatoes
  • 2 onions
  • Fresh pepper to taste
  • Tomato paste
  • Cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Seasoning cubes
  • Water or stock
  • Optional: thyme and curry powder

👩‍🍳 Cooking Instructions

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Blend the tomatoes, peppers and onions into a smooth mixture.
  3. Heat cooking oil in a pot and fry the tomato paste briefly.
  4. Add the blended mixture and cook until the sauce thickens.
  5. Add seasoning, salt, spices and stock or water as needed.
  6. Add the washed rice and stir to combine it with the sauce.
  7. Cover the pot and cook on low heat until the rice is tender, checking occasionally and adding a little water if necessary.
  8. Serve your delicious party jollof rice and enjoy!

Chef's Tip Keep the pot covered while the rice cooks, and use low heat to help it cook evenly without burning.
