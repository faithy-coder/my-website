🌆 Day 1: Arrival in Abuja

We arrived early and checked in. The city was beautiful, with impressive buildings, clean roads, and exciting places to explore.

"The best way to learn is to travel." — My Teacher

After settling in, we spent some time enjoying the surroundings and planning our activities for the next day.

📍 Day 2: Sightseeing

On the second day, we explored different places and learned more about the city.

  1. Aso Rock viewpoint: Enjoying the scenery around the famous landmark.
  2. National religious landmarks: Learning about important places of worship.
  3. A local market: Exploring local goods and everyday city life.

It was an enjoyable experience filled with new sights and memorable moments.
